# 09: Two steps in Topics: "Upload topics" then "Upload notes"

**What to build:** A course's Topics section has two step buttons. Step 1, Upload topics, offers two choices: from the course profile (ticket 08) or type them in (several at once, saved together). Step 2, Upload notes, runs the notes upload and AI check, and stays locked with "Add this course's topics first" until the course has topics. The next step is highlighted. Tapping a topic's level shows where in the notes it was found. Matches the approved prototype (artifact "Topics & Notes Prototype").

**Blocked by:** 08 (Import topics from a saved course profile)

**Status:** done

- [x] With no topics, Upload notes is disabled and the empty state explains the two steps
- [x] Typed topics are added in the order entered; duplicates are refused with a plain sentence
- [x] Edit is only for removing topics
- [x] An analysis reason like "pp. 12-15 · …" shows the location set apart from the sentence
