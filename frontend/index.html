Questions

Good to know

When can I start using it?

We're opening early access in small groups. Join the waitlist and we'll email you as soon as your spot is ready.

Does it cost anything?

Early access is free. If we introduce paid plans later, waitlist members will hear about it first.

Is my university supported?

Trajectory reads the course profile you upload or share, so it never needs to log in to your university's systems. We're starting with The University of Queensland, then adding the universities our waitlist asks for most.

Do I have to add my notes?

No, but it helps. Without notes, Trajectory works from your course's topics and your quiz results. With notes, it can see what you've already covered, page by page, and plan around the gaps.

Which notes can I add?

PDFs (including GoodNotes and OneNote exports: Share, then Save to Files), photos of handwritten pages, and Markdown or text files, up to 20 MB each. A whole semester's notebook is fine, even one that covers more than one course.

What happens to my notes?

They're read once to find and rate each topic, then deleted. We keep only a level for each topic, a one-line reason and where it appeared (like "pp. 12–15"), never the text of your notes, and we never sell or share them. See our Privacy Policy.
